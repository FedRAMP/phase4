## Notice of Intent - Potential Participants

The following cloud services have formally notified the FedRAMP Director of their intent to participate in the 20x Class D Pilot - they are not confirmed official participants yet, and must meet all eligibility requirements and complete a successful Pilot Proposal.

To be added to this list, you must be listed on the FedRAMP Marketplace and email the FedRAMP Director at pete@fedramp.gov to notify us of your intent to participate. **Notification of intent is not necessary** to apply, it simply helps us keep track.
