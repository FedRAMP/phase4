# 20x Class D Pilot Participation

Participation in the 20x Class D Pilot will be restricted to maximize efficiency and decrease risk. Cloud services
are eligible to apply for participation in the 20x Class D Pilot if they meet the following criteria:

1. The cloud service offering applied for a FedRAMP 20x Class C Certification before **December 11, 2026.**
2. The FedRAMP 20x Class C application is complete and sufficient, addressing all applicable requirements with appropriate implementations.
3. The FedRAMP 20x Class C application includes a broadly supportive independent verification and validation that is also complete and sufficient.
4. Agree to implement all FedRAMP 20x Class D Pilot requirements with independent verification and validation by **May 7, 2027.**

## Application

Eligible cloud service offerings will be able to apply to participate in the 20x Class D Pilot in the beginning of **January 2027.** Application
will require a presentation to FedRAMP demonstrating the readiness and executive support necessary to complete a 20x Class D Certification in the time alloted.

Interested and eligible providers should carefully review the entire application process and discuss it internally prior to applying.

## Application Form

FedRAMP will provide an application form in early December that will allow cloud service providers to sign up and prove eligibility. This form will require:

- Cloud service metadata including name, offering name, FedRAMP ID, etc.
- Name, title, and contact information for primary point of contact
- Email address for a distribution list managed by the cloud service provider for ongoing comms (we will not maintain lists of a bunch of email addresses to include on communications)

FedRAMP will review this information and contact you to schedule a Pilot Proposal.

## Pilot Proposals

[Pilot Proposals were a highlight of the FedRAMP 20x Phase 2 pilot](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL1orhY9kSkzQSN6_PiwZoyx0FaTfNE_MU), so we are bringing them back with minor changes!
Pilot Proposals help FedRAMP get to know you, learn about your company, and make an informed estimate of your ability to meet the requirements while providing meaningful feedback that will
benefit FedRAMP and all other stakeholders. They also publicly demonstrate commitment and support up front because you'll need to agree to FedRAMP sharing the proposal with the community.

For 20x Phase 4 Class D Pilot Proposals, we expect each company to bring the following participants:

- A senior executive sponsor (they don't have to present but we want to know you have the juice).
- Your GRC engineering and federal compliance team or equivalent (at least the senior folks).
- A representative from your independent assessment service (more than one is even better) - unlike the Phase 2 pilot proposals, the assessor will not be required to present but this is still an opportunity to showcase a strong working relationship.

!!! example "The FedRAMP Team"

    You can expect to be presenting to the FedRAMP Director (Pete), Security Director (Nicole), Chief of Staff (Ryan), Vanguard 20x Team Lead (Dan), and maybe a few other folks on the FedRAMP team.

    Be ready for both technical and non-technical questions from FedRAMP!

## Pilot Proposal Content

You will need to present the following information aligned with these general timelines:

1. **Introduction and Background** (~15 minutes)
    - Introduce your team, including your assessor, and say hello!
    - Provide a brief overview of your cloud service and history with FedRAMP.
    - Explain why you want to participate in the 20x Class D pilot, and why you are confident in providing that level of assurance to the government.
    - Tell us about your GRC environment, including a quick review of tools you are using vs building yourself.
2. **Class D Pilot Approach** (~30 minutes)
    - Demonstrate how you plan to approach each of the new Key Security Indicators.
    - Discuss any Class D-specific rules that you think are particularly interesting and how you will approach them.
3. **Q&A w/ FedRAMP** (~10 minutes)
    - The FedRAMP team will ask follow-up questions based on your proposal.

There is no template slide deck, specific order of approach for KSIs etc., or any such rigid structure. You should design this presentation to convey information most effectively for your service.


!!! tip "Don't worry too much about perfect presentation but do plan it out!"

    You won't be judged primarily on the quality and polish of your presentation, but we do want to see that you're taking this seriously and investing time and effort into the process. We strongly recommend that you practice as a team and get feedback from folks within your company (and maybe your assessor!) to smooth off any sharp edges so that the team is confident and ready to go on proposal day.

    There's a good chance we'll have proposals or other meetings scheduled back-to-back, so keep an eye on the clock and make sure you don't run over or we'll miss out on critical information!

!!! warning "This is not a sales pitch!"

    We don't really care about what your cloud service itself does, and we don't want to hear about that in depth. You are certainly welcome to talk about it in your introduction but we expect the majority of the proposal to focus on **how** you will approach the pilot.

    This applies even for GRC tools - we don't want to hear about how your GRC tool works or what it will do, we want to hear about the technical and philosophical approach you will bring to using it.
