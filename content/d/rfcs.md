# Phase 4 Requests for Comment and Notices

FedRAMP publishes formal Requests for Comment (RFCs) during the development of new guidance and strategies. These RFCs include FedRAMP's initial proposals for these updates but are never considered official or finalized guidance. FedRAMP is responsive to public comment and often changes the final outcome significantly based on public comment.

Public Notices (NTCs) are formal notifications about RFC outcomes and other intended FedRAMP actions or updates. Public Notices are intended to ensure stakeholders are clearly aware of critical activities that FedRAMP will take.
