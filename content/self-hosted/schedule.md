# Self-Hosted Industry Engagement Schedule

!!! warning "All dates are subject to change due to unanticipated events!"

Engagement with industry began in **October, 2026.**

| Start Date | End Date | Activities |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 2026-10-16 | 2026-10-23 | Providers eligible for direct engagement received an email from the FedRAMP Director via their FedRAMP Security Inbox. |
| 2026-10-27 | 2026-11-03 | Independent assessment services eligible for direct engagement will receive an email from the FedRAMP Director via their listed Marketplace email. |
| 2026-11-04 | 2026-12-05 | An RFC will be published with general questions about existing certifications for managing self-hosted or hybrid-hosted services. |
| 2026-11-04 | 2026-11-20 | FedRAMP will meet with industry directly to discuss aspects of the RFC and related questions. |
| 2027-01-04| 2027-01-15 | FedRAMP will meet again with industry directly to discuss the results of the RFC and the proposed final self-hosted and hybrid-hosted rules. |
| 2027-02-10 | 2027-03-12 | An RFC will be published with the proposed final self-hosted and hybrid-hosted rules. |
| 2027-03-15 | 2027-03-26 | FedRAMP will meet a third time with industry directly to discuss the final RFC. |
