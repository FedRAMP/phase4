# A-130 Class G Pilot Participation

The pilot is open to federal agencies that operate eligible shared cloud services for other agencies. Agency providers with an existing FedRAMP Marketplace listing and those seeking their first listing can both participate.

## Scope of Agency-Provided Offerings

To be eligible for a FedRAMP Class G Certification, agency-provided cloud service offerings must meet all of the following requirements:

1. The cloud service offering is within the scope of FedRAMP (especially, it is a shared service intended to be used by multiple agencies).
2. The cloud service offering is operated by an agency (including under direct contract where legally relevant).
3. The authorizing official, system owner, privacy official, and all other key RMF roles are filled by appropriate federal officials or contractors operating under authority and responsibility delegated from federal officials.

System owners who are unsure whether their offering meets these requirements should consult with appropriate counsel.

## Agency Class G Pilot Participation

If you are the system owner of an eligible offering, email the FedRAMP Director at [pete@fedramp.gov](mailto:pete@fedramp.gov) to express interest. FedRAMP will meet with your agency to discuss how the rules apply to your offering and help you plan the transition.

The pilot begins in January 2027. After a kickoff meeting with FedRAMP, your agency may begin its transition as early as December 2026. During the pilot, FedRAMP will work with participating agencies to check that the rules are clear and that customers have the information they need to use a shared service.
