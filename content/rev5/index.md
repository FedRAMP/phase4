# Rev5 Program Closeout

!!! danger "FedRAMP will close the pipeline for new Rev5 Certifications on **June 11, 2027**."

The FedRAMP Rev5 Program Closeout is a set of combined initiatives that will ensure that the majority of outstanding cloud service providers who have seriously invested in FedRAMP Rev5 will be able to receive a FedRAMP Rev5 Certification before FedRAMP stops accepting applications.

Each initiative has a different purpose, with different eligibility criteria and deadlines. Cloud services can only apply for Rev5 Program Certification if they meet the eligibility criteria for one of these initiatives and have completed all CR26 requirements prior to the submission deadline.

Some initiatives have pre-application and approval requirements. Please follow the instructions on the appropriate page before contacting FedRAMP.

!!! warning "Cloud Services MUST be Eligible for FedRAMP Certification!"

    To be listed in the FedRAMP Marketplace and qualify for FedRAMP Certification, cloud services must have one of the following government-wide use cases:

    1. **Direct Government-Wide Use:** The service will be used directly by multiple federal agency customers for integration into federal information systems that fall within the scope of 44 USC § 3506.

    2. **Indirect Government-Wide Use:** The service will be used as a third-party information resource in other cloud services that have direct government-wide use.

    Cloud services may not apply for any FedRAMP Rev5 Program Closeout initiative unless they can verify these use cases and demonstrate that the cloud service is within the scope of FedRAMP.

## :lucide-unlink:{ .xl .middle .empty } **Lost Sponsor**

If your offering lost its agency sponsor while listed as Agency Auth In Process, you may qualify for Lost Sponsor. You may also qualify if you completed a full FedRAMP assessment under an informal agreement with an agency to initiate sponsorship once the assessment was complete.

**Deadline for Lost Sponsor Program Certification:** March 26, 2027

Learn more at [Lost Sponsor](lost-sponsor.md){ data-preview }

---

## :lucide-clipboard-check:{ .xl .middle .empty } **Ready Conversion**

Ready Conversion gives providers a path from FedRAMP Ready to Rev5 Program Certification at full Class B or C.

**Deadline for Ready Conversion Program Certification:** March 26, 2027

Learn more at [Ready Conversion](ready.md){ data-preview }

---

## :lucide-monitor-cloud:{ .xl .middle .empty } **Li-SaaS Conversion**

If your offering held an active Rev5 Li-SaaS Certification in 2026 and you have been preparing for full Rev5 Class B or C Certification, you can apply for conversion with a complete CR26-compliant package.

**Deadline for Li-SaaS Conversion Program Certification:** April 26, 2027

Learn more at [Li-SaaS Conversion](li-saas.md){ data-preview }

---

## :lucide-flag:{ .xl .middle .empty } **Endgame Certification**

If your offering has an agency sponsor but the agency may miss the Rev5 cutoff, Endgame Certification allows eligible providers to transfer a late-stage review to FedRAMP. The agency can then use the Program Certification materials to complete its own authorization to operate.

**Deadline for Endgame Program Certification:** May 26, 2027

Learn more at [Endgame Certification](endgame.md){ data-preview }
