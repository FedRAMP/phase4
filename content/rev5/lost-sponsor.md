# Lost Sponsor

If your offering lost its agency sponsor or completed an assessment in anticipation of sponsorship, you may qualify to apply directly to FedRAMP for Rev5 Program Certification.

## Eligibility

Your offering must have met **at least one** of these conditions between January 2025 and December 2026:

- Your offering was Agency Auth In Process on the FedRAMP Marketplace and lost its agency sponsorship.
- Your offering completed a full FedRAMP assessment, including a Security Assessment Plan (SAP) and Security Assessment Report (SAR), under an informal agreement with an agency to initiate In Process status once the assessment was complete.

## Application Timelines

| Date | Action |
| -- | -- |
| December 15, 2026 | Pre-application window opens. |
| January 26, 2027 | Pre-application window closes. |
| March 26, 2027 | Final day for submitting a full CR26-compliant application for FedRAMP Certification |

!!! info "Already accepted?"

    If you have met with FedRAMP and been accepted into Lost Sponsor, you have completed the first step. Your Certification application is still due March 26.

## Pre-Application

You must schedule a discussion with FedRAMP to verify you are eligible to apply and notify us of your intent to submit an application for Program Certification:

- Use this form to begin the pre-application process
- Stand by for FedRAMP to contact you and schedule a meeting to review your materials and discuss eligibility

## Application Process

Follow the Consolidated Rules for 2026 to apply for a FedRAMP Rev5 Program Certification with a complete CR26-compliant Certification Package prior to March 26, 2027.
