# Endgame Certification

FedRAMP will close the FedRAMP Rev5 Certification pipeline entirely on June 11, 2027 with the intent of completing all outstanding reviews by the end of July 2027. All technical review staff will be realigned to focus entirely on FedRAMP 20x at that time - FedRAMP is not able to maintain two separate review teams running entirely differently processes concurrently past July 2027 because our contract agreement for Rev5 support staff will have ended.

Even with a year of advance warning, we anticipate that both agencies and cloud service providers will not be fully prepared for this deadline or that circumstances outside the agency’s control may lead to unexpected delays that prevent them from completing a review before this deadline.

The Endgame Certification initiative is intended to proactively address this problem by allowing the transfer of late-stage sponsored Agency Certifications over to FedRAMP to complete as a Program Certification; the agency can then complete its ATO aligned to agency priorities based on the materials in the FedRAMP Certification.

## Eligibility

Your offering must meet **all** of the following criteria.

1. **Marketplace Status:** Your offering must formally hold Agency Auth in Process status on the FedRAMP Marketplace by **March 1, 2027**.
2. **Certification Package:** You must have a complete CR26-compliant FedRAMP Rev5 Certification Package. If you completed your package under the legacy rules, you will need to update it to meet CR26 before applying for Program Certification.
3. **Sponsor Letter:** Your agency sponsor must provide a letter indicating that the agency may be unable to complete Agency Certification before **April 26, 2027**.

FedRAMP will perform a Program Certification at the same Certification Class that the original agency sponsor intended, including Class B, C, or D.

## Application Timelines

| Date | Action |
| -- | -- |
| March 1, 2027 | Pre-application window opens. |
| April 26, 2027 | Pre-application window closes. |
| May 26, 2027 | Final day for submitting a full CR26-compliant application for FedRAMP Certification |

## Pre-Application

You must work with FedRAMP in advance to transfer the initial FedRAMP Certification Path from Agency Certification to Program Certification. This process may begin no earlier than March 1, 2027 and should be completed by April 26, 2027.

- Use this form to begin the pre-application process (after March 1, 2027)
- Stand by for FedRAMP to contact you and schedule a meeting to review your materials and discuss eligibility

## Application Process

Follow the Consolidated Rules for 2026 to apply for a FedRAMP Rev5 Program Certification with a complete CR26-compliant Certification Package prior to May 26, 2027.
